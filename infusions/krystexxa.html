Specialty Infusion Center

Krystexxa Infusion Therapy

Physician-supervised Krystexxa (pegloticase) infusions for chronic, uncontrolled gout — in a private outpatient setting.

Book a Consultation Call 833-STEM561

About this therapy

Krystexxa is an infusion medication prescribed for adults with chronic gout that has not responded to conventional urate-lowering therapy. Treatment is administered in our monitored infusion suite, with pre-infusion screening and coordination with your prescribing physician at every step.

What every infusion visit includes

  • Pre-infusion screening and vitals check
  • Clinical monitoring throughout your infusion
  • A private, comfortable suite — bring headphones, a book, or just rest
  • Coordination with your prescribing physician on protocol and follow-up

Specialty infusions are administered on a physician's prescription. Our team helps coordinate referrals, insurance authorization, and scheduling — call 833-783-6561 to get started.

Getting scheduled

  • Have your prescription or referral ready
  • We verify insurance and authorization
  • Choose an appointment window that fits your week
