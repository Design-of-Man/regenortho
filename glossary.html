Regenerative Medicine Glossary PRP, exosomes, peptides, NAD+ and the rest, in plain words

The terms you will meet on our treatment pages and at your consultation, each with a short definition and a link to the page that explains the treatment in full.

C

Cold laser
Low-level laser light used, alongside shockwave and rehabilitation, to calm inflammation in tissue that has stopped healing on its own. Learn more about Cold laser

E

Endovenous ablation
Laser or radiofrequency heat delivered inside a diseased saphenous vein under ultrasound guidance. It seals the vein so blood reroutes to healthy vessels, treating the root cause of varicose veins. Learn more about Endovenous ablation
EPAT shockwave
Extracorporeal pulse activation technology: pressure waves applied through the skin to stimulate blood flow and collagen remodeling. Some soreness after a session is possible; most people return to normal activity right away. Learn more about EPAT shockwave
Exosomes
Naturally occurring extracellular vesicles rich in growth factors and signaling molecules. Exosome therapy is cell-free: it uses these vesicles, not living cells, to support the body’s healing response. Learn more about Exosomes

G

GHK-Cu
A copper-binding peptide the body makes on its own. The research on it centres on collagen and the extracellular matrix, which is why it is used when connective tissue is holding recovery back. Learn more about GHK-Cu
GLP-1 receptor agonist
A medication that mimics a naturally occurring hormone that regulates blood sugar, appetite and digestion. Semaglutide (Ozempic®, Wegovy®) and tirzepatide (Zepbound®) are the ones named on our weight loss page, prescribed only when appropriate for you. Learn more about GLP-1 receptor agonist
Glutathione
An antioxidant. On our IV menu it is part of the Beauty & Glow, Immune Boost and Cleanse drips. Learn more about Glutathione

H

HCT/P
Human cells, tissues, and cellular and tissue-based products: the FDA’s category (21 C.F.R. Part 1271) for products such as umbilical cord-derived cells. The cellular products used here are HCT/Ps from accredited U.S. suppliers; cell factors such as RPA are not classified as HCT/Ps.

K

KPV
A three-amino-acid fragment of a hormone the body already produces, studied for inflammatory regulation and immune balance in joint and soft-tissue recovery. Learn more about KPV

M

Mesenchymal stem cells
In the therapy offered here, cells derived from umbilical cord tissue and delivered by ultrasound-guided injection to support the body’s natural healing response in joints and soft tissue. Learn more about Mesenchymal stem cells
Microphlebectomy
Removal of superficial varicose veins through micro-incisions, done in the office, with a quick return to activity. Learn more about Microphlebectomy
MOTS-c
A peptide that comes from the mitochondria, studied for cellular energy, metabolic efficiency and endurance. Learn more about MOTS-c
MUSE cells
Multilineage-Differentiating Stress-Enduring cells: a rare population of mesenchymal stem cells. Traditional MUSE cell therapy is a live-cell treatment, given by IV push or targeted injection. Learn more about MUSE cells
Myers’ cocktail
A classic IV blend of vitamins and minerals. It is the base of several drips on our menu; Myers’ PLUS is a reinforced version. Learn more about Myers’ cocktail

N

NAD+
Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide, a coenzyme the body uses in cellular energy. Our NAD⁺ 500 mg infusion is a high-dose IV of it. Learn more about NAD+

O

Orthobiologics
Biologic injections used in orthopedic care, such as PRP and cell-based products, placed under ultrasound to support the body’s own repair of joints, tendons and ligaments. Learn more about Orthobiologics

P

Peptide therapy
Physician-supervised protocols using peptides, short chains of amino acids, matched to what you are trying to fix: repair, recovery, longevity or aesthetics. Learn more about Peptide therapy
PRP (platelet-rich plasma)
Growth factors concentrated from your own blood and placed at the injury, under ultrasound guidance. Learn more about PRP (platelet-rich plasma)

R

Regenerative medicine
Treatments that aim at the tissue itself rather than the symptom. They supply the signals the body already uses to repair tissue (growth factors, peptides, cellular material), concentrated and placed where the damage is. Learn more about Regenerative medicine
RPA (Regenerative Protein Array)
An acellular protein array. The MUSE-infused version is enhanced with proteins extracted from MUSE cells and given by IV push or injection. Learn more about RPA (Regenerative Protein Array)

S

Sclerotherapy
Targeted injections that close small surface veins, such as spider and reticular veins. Quick, in the office, with minimal recovery. Learn more about Sclerotherapy

T

Tesamorelin
A GHRH analog. Rather than giving you growth hormone, it prompts your own pituitary to release more of it; studied for lean-mass maintenance, visceral fat and recovery. Learn more about Tesamorelin

U

Ultrasound guidance
Live ultrasound imaging used to place an injection precisely, matched to what the imaging shows. Learn more about Ultrasound guidance

W

Wharton’s jelly
Umbilical cord tissue rich in naturally occurring growth factors, cytokines and extracellular matrix proteins, delivered by ultrasound-guided injection. Learn more about Wharton’s jelly

None of the therapies described are FDA-approved to treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, and they are not a substitute for indicated surgical care.
